## Takeaways

- **robotics.org.za** is the deepest, most professional catalog of the four — dedicated categories for nearly everything on the list (breadboards, jumper wires, rotary encoders, enclosures, batteries). Treat as the primary source.
- **nde3d.co.za** is a strong second, especially for RF modules and antennas specifically — good backup/alternate stock source.
- **pishop.co.za** is solid for the 433MHz kit (bundled with antennas, convenient) and has Adafruit stock for I2C/rotary parts, though an exact SKU for every item wasn't pinned down — worth a manual browse of their Adafruit and Sensors categories.
- **netram.co.za** has thinner coverage of this specific list — good for chargers, LEDs, connectors, and ESP32-C3 boards, but no dedicated breadboard/jumper-wire/rotary-encoder/enclosure listings turned up; likely not the go-to for this build.
- **MicroSD breakout** wasn't confirmed on any of the four — worth a manual site search, or treat as optional since SD logging on the hub is a "nice to have," not core to v1.
