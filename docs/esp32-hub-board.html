ESP32-S3 hub board

A handheld radio deck on one 18650 cell. An ESP32-S3 hub drives a 2.8" display and talks to three radios: an ESP32-C3 WiFi node, an ESP32-H2 BLE node and a CC1101 on 433 MHz, with GPS time and position fed to both nodes. This page has everything needed to buy the parts, place them, and wire each pin.

  • Board 170 × 100 mm (concept)
  • 2 layers, shared ground plane

Check before you order

  1. Power switch rating. Mini slide switches like the SS12D00 are usually rated 0.3–0.5 A, and the deck peaks around 1 A through the switch. Buy one rated at least 1 A.
  2. Nav module pin count. Most 5-way modules have 8 pins (COM, UP, DWN, LFT, RHT, MID, SET, RST), not 7. Count yours before choosing the footprint.
  3. BOM links. The C1 link goes to a 22 µF part and the C12 link goes to a 100 nF part. Check both listings.
  4. Onboard RGB LED. It's on GPIO48 on DevKitC-1 v1.0 and most clones, and GPIO38 on v1.1. GPIO38 is BTN_UP; on a v1.1 board the LED may flicker when you press up.
  5. Clone row spacing. Clone N16R8 boards are often wider than the Espressif board. Measure yours before placing the sockets.
  6. GPIO35–37. On N16R8 modules these are used by the octal PSRAM, so they must stay unconnected.
  7. Optional series resistors. 1 kΩ on the four hub-to-node UART lines protects the chips when you flash one node over USB while the deck is off.
